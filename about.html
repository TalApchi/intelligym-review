About

Soccer IQ

Soccer IQ is a website about soccer game intelligence: the ability to read the game, see what is about to happen and choose well under pressure.

What you'll find here

Five short challenges you can play in your browser, plain explanations of the skills behind reading the game, practical tips, a knowledge hub, and a comparison of soccer training tools.

What our scores mean

Soccer IQ Points describe how you did in our five games. They are not an IQ test, a validated assessment or a measure of how good a player you are. Some tactical situations are still being reviewed by a coach and are marked provisional.

How we compare training tools

Each tool is described from its own official pages, in the same format. The tools are different kinds of products, so the comparison shows how they differ rather than ranking them.

Your privacy

There are no accounts. Your latest challenge result is stored only in your own browser so the Your results page can show it; clearing your browser data removes it.

Coupons

Soccer IQ visitors can get a special IntelliGym coupon from the button in IntelliGym's profile on the Training tools page. It is redeemed on IntelliGym's own site, under IntelliGym's terms.
