FAQ

Questions and answers

About the Soccer IQ challenges, your scores and the training tools we compare.

What is Soccer IQ?

A soccer website about game intelligence: five short challenges, tips on reading the game, and a comparison of training tools.

Is my score an IQ test?

No. Soccer IQ Points describe how you did in these five games, out of 500. They are not an IQ test, a validated assessment or a prediction of how you play.

Do I need an account?

No. The challenges run in your browser. Your latest result is kept only on this device so you can look at it again on the Your results page.

What do I need to play?

A phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser. On a phone, tap; on a computer, click.

Do practice rounds count?

No. Demonstrations and practice tries are never scored. Each challenge is worth up to 100 points.

Why are some results marked provisional?

Some tactical situations are still being reviewed by a coach, so their scores can change.

Will playing the challenges improve my game?

We don't claim that. The challenges are short games that make you think about scanning, awareness, decisions and timing. For training, see the training tips and the training tools comparison.

Which training tools do you compare?

BlazePod, Be Your Best, IntelliGym, NeuroTracker and Rezzil. Each is described from its official pages in the same format. See the comparison table.

Is there a special IntelliGym coupon?

Yes. Use the "Get a special IntelliGym coupon" button in IntelliGym's profile on the Training tools page. The coupon is redeemed on IntelliGym's own site, under IntelliGym's terms.

Still curious?

Try the challenges and see how you read the game.
