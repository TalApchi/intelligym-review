Draft situations for review

Two sets: the Tactical choice scenarios in the game, then the Scan before receiving situations from the prototype playground.

Tactical choice: each play shows 3 of these situations, picked at random. Nothing here is approved yet: the positions, the options, which option is ranked best, the sentence a player reads afterwards and the reasoning were all written by the build team as drafts.

How to review
  1. Tap an option to see the pass or move play out on the picture.
  2. For each situation choose Approve, Needs change or Remove, and add a comment if anything is off (a position, a ranking, a sentence).
  3. Press Copy my review at the bottom and paste it into an email or message to Tal.

Your choices are kept in this browser only, so you can stop and come back on the same device.

Scoring and what has been checked

Draft scoring: best option 100 points, reasonable 50, weak 0; the challenge score is the average of the 3 situations. This scheme is also awaiting approval.

What the automated checks cover: players don't overlap, every pass ends at a player (or in the goal for a shot), every arrow belongs to a real player, each situation has 3 or 4 options and exactly one best, and the distances quoted in sentences match the picture. They say nothing about whether the football is right. That is your call.

Scan before receiving (prototype)

A playground experiment, not yet in the game. The player looks at the picture for 2 seconds; then the opponents disappear, the ball arrives, and they tap the teammate they'd pass to. Each play shows one situation, and none repeats until all have been played. Which pass is the draft best, which are reasonable, and the reasoning are drafts. The sentence under each pass is generated from the distances in the picture.
