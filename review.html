Draft situations for review

Two sets: the Tactical choice scenarios in the game, then the Opponents vanish situations (challenge 4, "Scan before receiving").

Tactical choice: each play shows 3 of these situations, picked at random. Nothing here is approved yet: the positions, the options, which option is ranked best, the sentence a player reads afterwards and the reasoning were all written by the build team as drafts.

How to review
  1. Tap an option to see the pass or move play out on the picture.
  2. For each situation choose Approve, Needs change or Remove, and add a comment if anything is off (a position, a ranking, a sentence).
  3. Press Copy my review at the bottom and paste it into an email or message to Tal.

Your choices are kept in this browser only, so you can stop and come back on the same device.

Scoring and what has been checked

Draft scoring: each option is ranked 100 (top), 75 (good), 50 (fair) or 25 (riskier), and two options can share a rank; the challenge score is the average of the 3 situations. This scheme is also awaiting approval.

Passing back or sideways: no rule scores a pass by its direction. Each option is ranked and explained from its own picture: pressure on you, how clear the lane is, how much space the receiver has, and what the pass keeps or opens.

What the automated checks cover: players don't overlap, every pass ends at a player (or in the goal for a shot), every arrow belongs to a real player, each situation has 3 or 4 options and at least one suggested option (more than one only with a note saying why), and the distances quoted in sentences match the picture. They say nothing about whether the soccer is right. That is your call.

Tactical choice

Opponents vanish (challenge 4)

The ball is played to the player, who looks at the picture while it travels (2.4, then 2.0, then 1.6 seconds); then the opponents disappear, the ball arrives, and they tap the teammate they'd pass to. Each play shows 3 situations, and none repeats until all have been played. Draft scoring: best 100 points, reasonable 50, weak 0. Which pass is the draft best, which are reasonable, and the reasoning are drafts. The sentence under each pass is generated from the distances in the picture.
