The tools

Be Your Best

Be Your Best AS, Oslo, Norway

A virtual reality app for Meta Quest headsets that puts players into first-person soccer match scenarios to train scanning, awareness and decision-making. A free phone companion app shows stats, replays and leaderboards but does not train on its own.

What it is
VR app with 800+ match scenarios: challenges, practice, an 11-a-side match mode and a career mode, with scanning metrics and replays.
Soccer-specific?
Fully soccer-specific: first-person match situations.
What you need
Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S or Pro headset. Companion app on iOS and Android.
Price
Personal plan (3 profiles): US$29 a month, or US$19 a month billed annually (US$228 a year). Club pricing on request.

BlazePod

Play Coyotta Ltd., Tel Aviv, Israel (US company: BlazePod Inc., Florida)

Wireless light-up pods that players tap or touch, run from a phone or tablet app with ready-made and custom reaction drills. It is used on the field, in the gym and in rehab across many sports; its soccer page promotes first-step speed, scanning and decision-making.

What it is
Physical reaction drills: pods light up and the player moves to tap them off; the app runs drills and records reaction times.
Soccer-specific?
No. A multi-sport and rehab product with a soccer page; the drills themselves are general reaction and movement drills.
What you need
BlazePod pods (kits of 3 to 12, with a charging base) and the free iOS or Android app. Space to move around the pods.
Price
Kits from US$219 (3 pods) to US$1,352 (12-pod team bundle) at regular prices. App: free Go tier; Pro US$12.99 a month or US$99.99 a year at regular prices.

The Soccer IntelliGym

IntelliGym Labs, New Jersey U.S

IntelliGym (ACE Applied Cognitive Engineering Ltd.)

Computer and iPhone software with game-like exercises that IntelliGym designed to train the mental side of soccer, such as anticipation, attention and awareness. No headset or special equipment is needed.

What it is
Game-like cognitive training exercises on screen, in 30-minute sessions.
Soccer-specific?
Made for soccer players, training skills IntelliGym links to the game, such as anticipation and awareness. IntelliGym says it "is not a video game".
What you need
Windows or Mac computer, or iPhone (iOS 12+). No Android version listed.
Price
Individual from $21.90 a month, or $15.90 a month on the annual plan ($190.80). Family and team plans available.

NeuroTracker

NeuroTracker (App Store seller: CogniSens Athletics Inc., Canada)

A screen-based trainer built on 3D multiple-object tracking: you follow several moving balls among distractors in 3D, and the speed adapts to you. It is used across many sports and is not specific to soccer.

What it is
3D multiple-object tracking task; difficulty and speed adapt to the user.
Soccer-specific?
No. The same generic task is used across sports and health; there is a soccer marketing page but no soccer content in the task.
What you need
Windows or Mac computer, web browser or tablet, with 3D glasses (one pair included with consumer plans).
Price
$30 a month, $20 a month billed annually, or $700 once. Currency and region not stated. Business plans on request.

Rezzil

Mi Hiepa Scout Ltd (trading as Rezzil), Manchester, UK

A Manchester VR company with two different offers: Rezzil Index, a platform for clubs and practitioners covering cognitive testing, training and rehab, and Rezzil Player, a consumer VR sports-drills game for PC VR and Meta Quest.

What it is
Rezzil Index: VR platform for clubs (testing, training, rehab, data). Rezzil Player: consumer VR game with 160+ drill levels.
Soccer-specific?
Mostly soccer for clubs, but described as multi-sport. Rezzil Player includes heading and goalkeeping drills alongside other sports.
What you need
VR headset. Rezzil Player: PC VR (SteamVR) or Meta Quest. Index hardware not published.
Price
Index: on request. Rezzil Player on Steam: $4.99 plus optional packs ($2.99 to $3.99). Meta Quest price could not be read.
